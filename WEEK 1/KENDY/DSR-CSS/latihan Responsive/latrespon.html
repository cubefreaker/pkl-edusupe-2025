The Islands

Indonesia is an archipelago country that has more than 17,000+++ Islands and 62% of sea area.

There are 7 main islands: Sumatra, Kalimantan, Jawa, Sulawesi, Papua, Kepulauan Maluku, and Kepulauan Sunda Kecil.
